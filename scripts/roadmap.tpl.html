About FET Lab

Explore FinFET, gate-all-around nanosheet, inner-wall forksheet and CFET examples. Compare device structures, illustrative CMOS inverters and schematic layouts, and step through how the FinFET and the nanosheet are made, from bare silicon to the finished device. Logic colours show ideal switching states, not simulated currents or timing.

Illustrative geometry, not a foundry recipe. One unit is 1 nm; Layout vertical dimensions are simplified and not to scale.
