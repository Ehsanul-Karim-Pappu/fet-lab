Reading the gate-all-around wrap. Slice across the channel (Z) and each sheet resolves into four concentric films — Si core, 1 nm SiO₂ interfacial layer, 2 nm HfO₂, then TiN — with Mo filling whatever is left between sheets. That 4 nm of Mo between adjacent TiN shells is the real scaling wall: shrink the sheet pitch further and the work-function metal from neighbouring sheets merges before the fill metal ever gets in. The Si₃N₄ slabs beside the gate are drawn as one body, which is how the outer spacer and the inner spacers appear once the cavity etch and refill are complete.
