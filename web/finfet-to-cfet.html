Technical references

References support device concepts, not this model's exact dimensions, complete material recipe or calculated capacitances. Geometry and colors are illustrative; no foundry PDK or measured device dataset is reproduced.

  1. R1 — Entering the nanosheet transistor era — imec

    FinFET, GAA nanosheet, inner-wall forksheet and process modules.

  2. R2 — Outer wall forksheet to bridge nanosheet and CFET device architectures in the logic technology roadmap — imec

    Inner-wall versus outer-wall forksheet; non-universal roadmap.

  3. R3 — Imec puts complementary FET (CFET) on the logic technology roadmap — imec

    Monolithic/sequential integration and post-transfer thermal constraints.

  4. R4 — Imec demonstrates functional monolithic CFET devices with stacked bottom and top contacts — imec

    Frontside contacts and advantages of backside contact formation.

  5. R5 — Advancing the CFET-based device roadmap: novel integration modules and standard-cell configurations — imec

    Common-gate and split-gate CFET options.

  6. R6 — Samsung Begins Chip Production Using 3nm Process Technology With GAA Architecture — Samsung Electronics, 30 June 2022

    Initial 3nm GAA production announcement.

  7. R7 — Intel's Transistor Technology Breakthrough Represents Biggest Change to Computer Chips In 40 Years — Intel, 28 January 2007

    Hafnium-based high-k/metal gates at 45nm.

  8. R8 — Ab initio calculation of Effective Work Functions for a TiN/HfO2/SiO2/Si transistor stack — Research paper, arXiv:1112.2163 (2011)

    Example gate stack; effective work function depends on the stack.

  9. R9 — Epi Source/Drain Damage Mitigation with Inner Spacer and Buffer Optimization in Stacked Nanosheet Gate-All-Around Transistors — IBM Research, SSDM 2023

    SiGe pFET source/drain epitaxy and inner-spacer integration.

  10. R10 — Analysis and Design of Digital Integrated Circuits: Problem Set 1 Solutions — MIT OpenCourseWare, 6.374 (2003)

    Electrical inverter balance is not determined by width alone.

  11. R11 — Work Function Engineering with Molybdenum and Molybdenum-Nitride Gate Electrodes — Rochester Institute of Technology repository

    Molybdenum as a researched gate material, not validation of this model's complete stack.

  12. R12 — A view on the logic technology roadmap — imec

    FEOL/MOL/BEOL, routing and standard-cell track height.

  13. R13 — Single stack dual channel gate-all-around nanosheet with strained PFET and bottom dielectric isolation NFET (US 2023/0420457 A1; granted as US 12,568,683 B2) — IBM (assignee), US patent

    The nanosheet process, Figs. 1–19, and the Si/SiGe CMOS Device and Inverter scenes. One Si/SiGe stack over a SiGe base (about 50 % Ge) makes an nFET with Si channels over bottom dielectric isolation and a pFET with SiGe channels (about 25 % Ge) on an n-type punch-through stopper; SiBCN-class spacer film that also fills the BDI cavity; region-by-region spacer etch-back and source/drain epitaxy; low-κ inner spacers; separate work-function metals, W fill, gate cut and SiN SAC caps, or a shared gate (Fig. 19). The full application and all its figures are in docs/nsfet. A disclosed integration, not evidence of any foundry's flow.

  14. R14 — A Novel Dry Selective Etch of SiGe for the Enablement of High Performance Logic Stacked Gate-All-Around NanoSheet Devices — IBM Research (Loubet et al.), IEDM 2019

    Two distinct uses of selective SiGe etch: a partial lateral recess at the exposed ends before inner-spacer fill, and the later channel release in the gate cavity. Its selectivity and device results belong to the studied process, not to this model.

  15. R15 — Full Bottom Dielectric Isolation to Enable Stacked Nanosheet Transistor for Low Power and High Performance Applications — IBM Research (Zhang et al.), IEDM 2019

    Why bottom isolation matters: compares full BDI with a punch-through-stopper scheme for sub-channel leakage and effective capacitance. Not evidence that its sequence matches R13's.

  16. R16 — Lithography principles — ASML

    A mask or reticle pattern is projected into photoresist; exposure changes the resist's solubility, development leaves protected and open regions, and a separate etch transfers the pattern into the film below.

  17. R17 — Six crucial steps in semiconductor manufacturing — ASML

    Deposition, resist coating, lithography, development, etch and ion implantation as the repeating steps that build a chip.

  18. R18 — Nanosheet technology for the computing era of AI and 5G — IBM Research

    Conceptual support for adjustable sheet width, growth-controlled sheet thickness, full BDI under gate and source/drain, and tight selective-etch control.

  19. R19 — First EUV lithography high-volume manufacturing solution for N5 BEOL — imec

    A back-end metal-line example of SAQP mechanics: core lines, first spacer and etch, core removal, reuse as the second core, second spacer and core removal, transfer to a hard mask, then a separate block pattern. An illustration of SAQP, not evidence of how R13 patterns its layers.

  20. R20 — Advanced in-line metrology strategy for self-aligned quadruple patterning — IBM Research

    Repeated sidewall-spacer image transfers give roughly a quarter of the original pitch in a periodic array, with accumulated variation such as pitch walk and CD variation.

  21. R21 — Spacer defined double patterning for sub-72 nm pitch logic technology — IBM Research

    Mandrels and a single spacer-defined pitch split (SADP).

  22. R22 — N7 FinFET Self-Aligned Quadruple Patterning Modeling (Baudot, Guissi, Milenin, Ervin, Schram) — imec and Coventor, SISPAD 2018 (author paper)

    Its Fig. 1 is an SAQP fin-patterning sequence: carbon cores at 96 nm pitch, an oxide first spacer, core removal, transfer of the first image into an amorphous-Si second core at 48 nm, a second spacer cycle to a 24 nm fin pitch, and transfer into silicon through a silicon nitride hard mask before STI; it studies pitch walk, core taper and fin-height variation. A research example, not the app's 108/54/27 nm values.

  23. R23 — SAQP Specs for 7nm finFETs — Semiconductor Digest

    Secondary account of SAQP for 7 nm-class FinFET fins and the core-dimension control it needs to keep pitch walk acceptable.

  24. R24 — FinFet device and method of forming the same (US 10,505,021 B2) — US patent

    A gate-last FinFET route in its written description and flowchart (Fig. 23): substrate with optional p- and n-wells and a first mask (Fig. 2A), fin etch (3A), isolation fill and CMP (4A), STI recess (5A), dummy gate dielectric, silicon and mask (6A/B), patterned dummy gate with optional lightly doped extensions (7A–C), selectively deposited gate spacers (8–15), source/drain fin recess (16A–C), epitaxial source/drain (17A–C), etch-stop layer, ILD and CMP (18A–C), dummy-gate removal (19A–C), replacement gate dielectric and metal (20A–C) and contacts (21A–C); Fig. 22 is a merged-epitaxy alternative. Mapped from the text; drawings not compared.

  25. R25 — Dummy fin structures and methods of forming same (US 10,510,580 B2) — US patent

    An integrated FinFET flow with gate seal and spacer films formed by deposition and anisotropic etch, nFET and pFET source/drain recess and epitaxy each with the other region masked, etch-stop layer and ILD, dummy-gate removal, replacement gate and contacts with optional silicide. Its dummy fins are deliberately fabricated structures, not the extra mask line the app cuts.

  26. R26 — Contacts for a fin-type field-effect transistor (US 9,741,615 B1) — US patent

    In self-aligned contact schemes the gate conductor needs a dielectric cap and sidewall isolation so a source/drain contact cannot short to it.

  27. R27 — Bulk finFET with punchthrough stopper region and method of fabrication (US 9,082,853 B2) — US patent

    A punch-through stopper in the lower fin, including an option where dopant is supplied from an STI liner and diffused by anneal: why STI between fins does not by itself control leakage beneath the channel. One separately disclosed option, not a step of R24's route.

  28. R28 — Nanosheet epitaxy with full bottom isolation (US 2023/0178617 A1) — IBM (applicant), US patent application

    An example Si/Si nanosheet CMOS route in Figs. 2–52: a Ge-rich sacrificial bottom layer, a Si seed layer, then lower-Ge SiGe and Si channel layers; stack lines, STI, sacrificial gates, spacers, source/drain recess, SiGe indent and inner spacers; undoped Si grown from the seed and channel ends; the Ge-rich layer removed and replaced by bottom dielectric isolation under both devices; pFET SiGe:B and nFET SiC:P source/drain each with the other region protected; ILD, sacrificial-gate removal, SiGe removal releasing the Si channels of both devices, and separate work-function metals. One disclosed example, not a production foundry flow; the Si seed layer stays only under the inner spacers and the undoped Si; under the gate it is oxidized and etched at channel release, and the gate sits on the bottom isolation. PDF in docs/nsfet.

  29. R29 — FinFET structures and methods of forming the same (US 9,812,358 B1) — Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (assignee), US patent

    The FinFET process, Figs. 2–22C, and the FinFET Device and Inverter scenes: fins, FCVD oxide STI and wells; dummy gate, gate seal spacers and LDD; dummy spacers, recess and SiP/SiGeB epitaxy by region; SiN gate spacers; dummy ILD, gate-last replacement gate (high-κ, electrodes from TiN, TaN, TaC, Co, Ru, Al), gate recess and metal-oxide hard mask; spin-on-carbon dummy contacts, tri-layer lithography, ILD and replacement contacts (liner, conductor, silicide). PDF in docs/finfet.

  30. R30 — Methods for fabricating integrated circuits using self-aligned quadruple patterning (US 9,171,764 B2) — GlobalFoundries (assignee), US patent

    The FinFET's SAQP fin patterning, Figs. 1–11: SiN hard masks, amorphous-Si upper and lower mandrels, SiN spacers about 14 nm and 10–20 nm, the lower spacers as the fin mask, variably spaced fin pairs and SRAM fin removal. PDF in docs/finfet.

  31. R31 — A method for forming a semiconductor device and a semiconductor device (EP 3 989 273 A1) — imec (applicant), European patent application

    The forksheet Device scene: Si/SiGe stacks either side of a SiN (or SiCO, SiCN, SiOCN) insulating wall 8–20 nm wide, based in the substrate and rising above the top channel; SiC or SiBCN gate spacers, SiN or low-κ inner spacers; P- and N-doped Si source/drain by region; a contact partition wall 10–24 nm wide on the insulating wall; TiN and W contacts in a merged opening; HfO2, TiN or TaN and TiAl or TiAlC work-function metals, W, Al, Co or Ru fill. PDF in docs/fsfet.

  32. R32 — Semiconductor device structure including forksheet transistors and methods of forming the same (US 11,862,700 B2) — Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (assignee), US patent

    A second forksheet route: a two-layer dielectric wall (high-κ then low-κ), a SiGe cladding, reduced end-cap spacing with air gaps between the channels, n-type and p-type gate electrode layers (TiAlC and TiN among others), a metal layer, SAC layer and silicided S/D contacts. PDF in docs/fsfet.

  33. R33 — Stacked complementary field effect transistors (US 11,869,812 B2) — IBM (assignee), US patent

    The monolithic CFET Device and Inverter scenes: one Si/SiGe stack on an insulating layer, the high-Ge SiGe between the tiers replaced by spacer material (SiBCN and others); a lower SiGe:B source/drain notched and isolated by an oxide layer, an upper Si:P source/drain above it; one HKMG (HfO2, TiN, TiAlC, W) with a gate dielectric cap; front-side contacts only, including a common via to both source/drains and a lower contact through the space a TiOx sacrificial spacer left. PDF in docs/cfet.

  34. R34 — CFETs and the methods of forming the same (US 2024/0413156 A1) — Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (applicant), US patent application

    The sequential CFET Device scene: a lower transistor made in one wafer, an etch stop, a dielectric with an inter-metal line (Cu) and SiO2 bond layers, a second wafer fusion-bonded and the upper transistor made in it; deep contact plugs from the front and the back to the inter-metal line; front- and backside interconnect; SiOCN inner spacers, TiAl and TiN work-function metals. PDF in docs/cfet. Its Chinese family member, CN 118712136 A (same applicant, inventors, figures and text), is in docs/cfet and is text-searchable.

  35. R35 — Semiconductor device structure including forksheet transistors and methods of forming the same (US 2024/0178128 A1) — Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (applicant), US patent application

    Another way to join a forksheet's gates, described in the forksheet story but not drawn: n and p gate electrodes separated by the dielectric wall and planarised level with it (gate height 15 nm or less above the top channel); a gate bridge contact (barrier TiN, fill W and others), wider than the wall, on the wall and in contact with both gates; the gate via contact landing on it; SiN etch stops, CESL and silicided S/D contacts with a TiN barrier. PDF in docs/fsfet.
